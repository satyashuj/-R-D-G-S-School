Home › About Us › History › R D G S School History

R D G S School, Auraiya is one of the most distinguished CBSE schools in the city, committed to providing holistic education for the all-round development of students. The dedicated team of educators fosters individual growth, leadership skills, discipline and social responsibility. The school continues to inspire learners to contribute positively to society and the nation.

Our Journey

Since its establishment, the institution has focused on academic excellence, innovation, sports and personality development. Thousands of students have successfully graduated and achieved success.

Our Vision

We believe in nurturing responsible citizens with strong moral values, creativity, leadership qualities and global awareness for a brighter tomorrow.

R D G S School

Excellence in education, innovation and discipline.
